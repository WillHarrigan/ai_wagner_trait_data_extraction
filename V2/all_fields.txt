# Wagner Trait Ontology — all traits and fields

15 groups · 24 categorical traits · 100 fields


## taxon_identity
   Identity of the organism — names and major-group classification (the actual taxon info, not document metadata).

   CATEGORICAL TRAITS (1):
     - description                          (4 values) — Major plant group classification (infer from taxonomy)

   FIELDS (3):
     - common_name                          [text] — Common or vernacular name of the plant
     - hawaiian_name                        [text] — Hawaiian name of the plant, including diacritical marks
     - infraspecific_epithet                [text] — Infraspecific taxon name following the species name

## source_document
   Non-trait document / provenance metadata — page number and paths to the species' images in the parsed source file.

   FIELDS (2):
     - plate_references                     [list] — Plate references for this species as they appear in the passage (e.g. 'Plate 7'). Case-insensitive; ignore markdown formatting like *Plate 7* or **plate 7**.
     - wagner_pg_number                     [text] — Reference page number in Wagner's Manual of Flowering Plants of Hawaii

## life_form
   Growth habit / life form and reproductive (breeding) class.

   CATEGORICAL TRAITS (2):
     - breeding_type                        (6 values) — Plant reproductive class (infer from taxonomy)
     - life_form_type                       (10 values) — Growth habit or life form (annual herb, perennial herb, shrub, tree, vine, etc.)

## reproductive_morphology
   Cytology — ploidy level and chromosome number.

   FIELDS (2):
     - chromosome_number                    [list]  [struct=chromosome] — Species specific chromosome count (integer number)
     - ploidy                               [list]  [struct=ploidy] — Ploidy level for the specific species expressed as a function of n (e.g., 1n, 2n or 3n, etc..)

## distribution
   Where the plant occurs (Hawaiian islands), its origin, and conservation status.

   CATEGORICAL TRAITS (3):
     - island_type                          (9 values) — Hawaiian island(s) on which the plant is found.
     - origin                               (4 values) — Origin abbreviated at the beginning of the passage (end/endemic, nat/naturalized, PC/paleotropical cosmopoltian.)
     - status                               (5 values) — Conservation or rarity status

## stem_morphology
   Stem / overall plant height and stem indumentum (hair type).

   CATEGORICAL TRAITS (1):
     - stem_hair_type                       (13 values) — Describes the stem hair type of the main stem (NOT penduncle) (e.g., glabrous, hirsute, tomentose, etc.)

   FIELDS (1):
     - stem_height                          [quantitative]  [struct=stem] — Overall height of the plant or length of the main stem

## leaf_morphology
   Leaf structure, shape, margin, phyllotaxy, and leaf/petiole dimensions. Covers WHOLE-LEAF traits (use leaflet_morphology for individual leaflets of compound leaves, and leaf_indumentum for leaf-surface hair detail).

   CATEGORICAL TRAITS (4):
     - leaf_margin_type                     (4 values) — Type of leaf edge (entire, serrate, lobed, etc.)
     - leaf_shape_type                      (33 values) — Shape of leaf blade (ovate, lanceolate, cordate, etc.)
     - leaf_type                            (2 values) — Leaf structure type (simple or compound)
     - phyllotaxy_type                      (8 values) — The arrangement of leaves around the stem (words like pairs and leaflet information are important indicators).

   FIELDS (2):
     - leaf_dimensions                      [quantitative]  [struct=leaf] — Dimensions of leaves or individual leaf blade
     - petiole_length                       [quantitative]  [struct=petiole] — Length of leaf stalk (petiole).

## leaf_indumentum
   Hair type on leaf surfaces (overall, upper, lower). Applies when the passage describes leaf-surface indumentum/pubescence — independent of leaf_morphology, which covers shape/margin/dimensions.

   CATEGORICAL TRAITS (3):
     - leaf_hair_type                       (13 values) — Type of leaf surface indumentum.
     - lower_leaf_hair_type                 (13 values) — Specific description of lower leaf surface indumentum.
     - upper_leaf_hair_type                 (13 values) — Specific description of upper leaf surface indumentum.

## leaflet_morphology
   Morphology of individual leaflets in compound leaves. Applies when the passage explicitly describes LEAFLETS (use leaf_morphology for whole-leaf descriptions, including leaves described as "lobed" or "dissected" — those are NOT leaflets).

   CATEGORICAL TRAITS (3):
     - leaflets_leaf_margin_type            (4 values) — Margin type of leaflets
     - leaflets_leaf_shape_type             (33 values) — Shape of individual leaflets
     - leaflets_leaf_type                   (2 values) — Leaflet leaf structure type in compound leaves

   FIELDS (1):
     - leaflets_leaf_dimensions             [quantitative]  [struct=leaflets_leaf] — Dimensions of individual leaflets

## juvenile_leaf_morphology
   Morphology of juvenile (early-stage) leaves. Applies when the passage explicitly describes juvenile or seedling leaves — independent of leaf_morphology, which covers adult leaves.

   CATEGORICAL TRAITS (4):
     - juvenile_leaf_hair_type              (13 values) — Indumentum type on juvenile leaves
     - juvenile_leaf_margin_type            (4 values) — Margin type of juvenile leaves
     - juvenile_leaf_shape_type             (33 values) — Shape of juvenile leaves
     - juvenile_leaf_type                   (2 values) — Leaf structure type in juvenile plants

   FIELDS (1):
     - juvenile_leaf_dimensions             [quantitative]  [struct=juvenile_leaf] — Dimensions of juvenile leaves

## inflorescence_morphology
   Inflorescence type and the dimensions of its parts (peduncle, pedicel, rachis, hypanthium, umbellet).

   CATEGORICAL TRAITS (1):
     - inflorescence_type                   (17 values) — Type of inflorescence

   FIELDS (13):
     - hypanthium_dimensions                [quantitative]  [struct=hypanthium] — Floral cup (hypanthium) dimensions
     - inflorescence_dimensions             [quantitative]  [struct=inflorescence] — Dimensions of the inflorescence.
     - pedicel_dimensions                   [quantitative]  [struct=pedicel] — Individual flower stalk (pedicel) in an inflorescence dimensions
     - peduncle_dimensions                  [quantitative]  [struct=peduncle] — Main inflorescence (peduncle) stalk dimensions
     - pistillate_inflorescence_dimensions  [quantitative]  [sex=female, struct=inflorescence] — Female inflorescence dimensions
     - pistillate_pedicel_dimensions        [quantitative]  [sex=female, struct=pedicel] — Individual flower stalk (pedicel)  in an inflorescence of female/pistillate plant dimensions
     - pistillate_peduncle_dimensions       [quantitative]  [sex=female, struct=peduncle] — Main inflorescence (peduncle) stalk of female/pistillate plant dimensions
     - rachis_diameter                      [quantitative]  [pos=diameter, struct=rachis] — Main inflorescence stalk (rachis) diameter
     - rachis_length                        [quantitative]  [struct=rachis] — Main inflorescence stalk (rachis) length
     - staminate_inflorescence_dimensions   [quantitative]  [sex=male, struct=inflorescence] — Male inflorescence dimensions
     - staminate_pedicel_dimensions         [quantitative]  [sex=male, struct=pedicel] — Individual flower stalk (pedicel) in an inflorescence of male/staminate plant dimensions
     - staminate_peduncle_dimensions        [quantitative]  [sex=male, struct=peduncle] — Main inflorescence (peduncle) stalk of male/staminate plant dimensions
     - umbellet_length                      [quantitative]  [struct=umbellet] — Secondary umbel unit length

## inflorescence_specific_morphology
   Specialised inflorescence parts — head/capitulum, pappus, rays, florets, spathe, spadix. Applies when the passage describes any of these specialised structures. Independent of inflorescence_morphology, which covers the inflorescence as a whole.

   FIELDS (10):
     - floret_color                         [text]  [struct=floret] — Color of florets (must specify florets vs flower/petal color)
     - florets_length                       [quantitative]  [struct=florets] — Length of small flowers that compose a flower head (capitulum)
     - head_diameter                        [quantitative]  [pos=diameter, struct=head] — Capitulum diameter
     - head_length                          [quantitative]  [struct=head] — Capitulum length
     - pappus_length                        [quantitative]  [struct=pappus] — Pappus length.
     - ray_color                            [text]  [struct=ray] — Ray floret color
     - ray_dimensions                       [quantitative]  [struct=ray] — Describes dimensions of rays or ray florets.
     - spadix_length                        [quantitative]  [struct=spadix] — Length of spadix
     - spathe_color                         [text]  [struct=spathe] — Color of spathe
     - spathe_dimensions                    [quantitative]  [struct=spathe] — Dimensions of spathe

## bract_involucre_morphology
   Bract, bracteole, and involucre morphology. Applies when the passage describes bracts or involucre. Independent of inflorescence_morphology.

   FIELDS (7):
     - bract_dimensions                     [quantitative]  [struct=bract] — Bract dimensions.
     - bract_lower_length                   [quantitative]  [pos=lower, struct=bract] — Lower/basal bract length
     - bract_outer_length                   [quantitative]  [pos=outer, struct=bract] — Outer bract length
     - bracteoles_dimensions                [quantitative]  [struct=bracteoles] — Dimensions of bracteoles
     - involucre_dimensions                 [quantitative]  [struct=involucre] — Involucre dimensions
     - pistillate_involucre_length          [quantitative]  [sex=female, struct=involucre] — Length of involucre of pistillate/female heads.
     - staminate_involucre_length           [quantitative]  [sex=male, struct=involucre] — Length of involucre of staminate/male heads.

     - fruit_type                           (7 values) — Type of  mature, ripened ovary of a flowering plant or fruit described.

   FIELDS (8):
     - bur_length                           [quantitative]  [struct=bur] — Bur (spiny fruit covering) length
     - fruit_diameter                       [quantitative]  [pos=diameter, struct=fruit] — Fruit diameter

     - seed_diameter                        [quantitative]  [pos=diameter, struct=seed] — Seed diameter
     - seed_length                          [quantitative]  [struct=seed] — Seed length.

     - seeds_perfruit                       [quantitative]  [struct=fruit] — Number of seeds per fruit
